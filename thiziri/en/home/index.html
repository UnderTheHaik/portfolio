أذكار الدخول إلى المنزل

بِسْـمِ اللهِ وَلَجْنـا، وَبِسْـمِ اللهِ خَـرَجْنـا، وَعَلـى رَبِّنـا تَوَكّلْـنا.

In the name of Allah we enter and leave, and upon our Lord we rely.

Reference:

Source and review note

Mentioning Allah on entering is established in Sahih Muslim 2018a. This longer entrance/exit wording is reported separately and has not been authenticated in this review.

أذكار الخروج من المنزل

بِسْمِ اللهِ ، تَوَكَّلْـتُ عَلى اللهِ وَلا حَوْلَ وَلا قُـوَّةَ إِلاّ بِالله. اللّهُـمَّ إِنِّـي أَعـوذُ بِكَ أَنْ أَضِـلَّ أَوْ أُضَـل ، أَوْ أَزِلَّ أَوْ أُزَل ، أَوْ أَظْلِـمَ أَوْ أَُظْلَـم ، أَوْ أَجْهَلَ أَوْ يُـجْهَلَ عَلَـيّ.

In the name of Allah. I rely upon Allah; there is no strength or power except through Allah. O Allah, I seek refuge in You from straying or being led astray, slipping or being made to slip, wronging or being wronged, and acting ignorantly or having ignorance directed against me.

Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud 5095 · Sunan Abi Dawud 5094

Source and review note

The reliance and refuge supplications are transmitted separately on leaving home.
