INSIDE THE PROJECT

ContextProblemApproachDesignDevelopmentChallengesResultWhat I learned
01

Context

My personal writing space for essays on faith, heritage, society, technology and everyday life. This is an independent personal project.

02

Problem

Long-form essays need a comfortable reading experience, a clear subject structure and a way to navigate between English and French versions.

03

Approach

Use content collections for articles, individual essay pages, subject archives and language-aware navigation. Separate the writing content from the shared presentation.

04

Design

An editorial masthead, restrained palette and generous reading width put the words first. Article images support the subject, with captions and credits where applicable. A dark reading mode is available.

05

Development

Astro generates static pages from typed Markdown content. Responsive WebP images, RSS, sitemap generation and page metadata support publishing and discovery. TypeScript helps keep content and route handling consistent.

06

Challenges

Translations, article metadata and image sources need to remain aligned as the journal grows. Reading layouts must also work with long titles and different image proportions.

07

Result

A published bilingual journal with essay and category pages, responsive imagery and a repeatable content workflow. Some editorial illustrations are AI-generated and identified in the journal documentation.

08

What I learned

A content website needs a maintainable publishing workflow as much as a strong homepage. Shared layouts and structured metadata make future updates more reliable.
