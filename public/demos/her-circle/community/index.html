THE IDEA BEHIND HER CIRCLE

A little less scrolling.
A little more living.

We’re a women-only community built around a simple idea: making new friends feels easier when there’s something lovely to do together.

CONNECTION, AT YOUR OWN PACE

You don’t have to
know anyone yet.

Maybe you’re new to the city. Maybe your friends are always busy. Maybe you simply want to try something different with people who are glad you came.

Her Circle makes space for the first hello and the familiar face. Join one gathering or become a regular. There’s no pressure to do everything.

WHAT MATTERS HERE

A warm welcome.
A thoughtful community.

Come as you are

Women from different backgrounds, interests and walks of life are welcome. You don’t need to fit a particular style.

Care for one another

Respect boundaries, make room for different voices, and ask before sharing someone’s photo or contact details.

Keep it easy

Clear plans, friendly introductions and no pressure to attend every event. Friendship should feel natural.

Your people might
be one plan away.
