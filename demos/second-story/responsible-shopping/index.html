Secondhand shopping starts with a different question: what’s already out there? A piece doesn’t need to be brand new to become your favourite.

Buy what you’ll actually wear, look closely at fabric and condition, and take your time finding the right fit. A good find is one that earns its place in your wardrobe.

01 / CHOOSE

Find your fit.

Try it on. Check seams, fastenings and soles. Choose comfort and quality over an impulse.

02 / CARE

Wear it longer.

Follow care labels, mend small faults, and store your pieces carefully.

03 / CIRCULATE

Pass it on.

If it no longer works for you, let it work for someone else. We buy secondhand too.
