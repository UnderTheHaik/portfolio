A clear plan for updates

A business website changes as your services, prices and photographs change. Optional website care gives you a defined allowance for those updates and a regular check of links and contact details. It is intended for the websites I deliver, with scope confirmed before ongoing work starts.

Website Care is 150 AED per month and includes a source backup, monthly link and contact checks, and up to 45 minutes of content updates in one monthly batch. Care Plus is 300 AED per month and includes up to two hours of updates in two monthly batches. Reviewing, editing, checking and publishing all count towards the allowance.

Unused time does not roll over. Routine updates are handled within three working days, and care can be cancelled before the next billing month. New pages, custom functionality and larger changes need a separate quote. If you prefer occasional updates, pay-as-you-go changes start at 100 AED for up to 30 minutes, with additional time at 75 AED per 30 minutes.

Before you begin

What about domain renewals?

Renewal management is 100 AED per year plus provider charges. The management fee is waived while monthly care is active; provider charges still apply.

Can I use the allowance for a promotional banner?

A simple promotional banner can use the Care Plus allowance. More extensive design or functionality is quoted separately.
