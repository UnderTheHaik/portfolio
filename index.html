The domain is registered in your name. Paid hosting is additional. Setup does not include maintenance.

Included with every build

Prices cover static websites in one language, client-supplied text and photographs, and an agreed design direction. Every build includes 14 days of corrections for faults in my delivered work. Content changes use the update rates. Social setup uses your existing branding; ongoing posts, ads and account management are separate.

Website care terms

Care is optional and can be cancelled before the next billing month. Time includes reviewing, editing, checking and publishing. Care includes one monthly batch; Care Plus includes two. Work quoted separately

Custom booking systems, checkout, user accounts, extensive animation and copywriting need a separate quote. Extras above use the approved design and stated time limits. Any new work is agreed before it begins.

  5. 05↗

  6. 06↗

    Each website revision round uses one consolidated list of changes. One-page websites include one round; business websites and logo work include two.

    After final payment, you receive the agreed files, account access and instructions for requesting updates.

BEFORE WE BEGIN

Your website questions, answered.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every website includes a responsive layout, with the main pages and visitor journeys checked on desktop and phone screens.

How much does a website cost?

Regular prices are 650 AED for a one-page website, 1,200 AED for a business website and 1,700 AED for Complete launch. The launch offer takes 50% off these packages: 325, 600 and 850 AED respectively. Domain setup, care, extras and provider charges are separate unless included in your chosen bundle.

How do we get started?

Ready to begin? Send me your business details, the pages you need, your budget and your preferred launch date. We agree on a written scope and quote before work starts.

Can you create my logo and colours too?

Yes. Starter visual identity is 500 AED regularly, or 250 AED with the launch offer. It includes a logo, colour palette, font pairing, one-page brand guide, two initial directions and two revision rounds on the chosen direction.

What happens after launch?

You receive the agreed files and account access. You have 14 days to report faults in the agreed work. Content updates are quoted before work begins.
