Health coaching homepage

The brief and approach

Context

A self-initiated portfolio concept combining two supplied references: a calm yoga website and a colorful food layout. The working brand, sample recipes and coaching offer are illustrative.

Problem

Present home, gym and outdoor training alongside personalized food guidance and a recipe blog within one coherent website.

Approach

Connect the coaching offer to a practical journal through a shared identity, clear navigation and simple exploration tools.

Development

Responsive layouts include training-location options, journal category filters, complete recipe dialogs, FAQs and a downloadable coaching enquiry.

Result

An integrated health coach and food blog demo with laptop and phone previews. No enquiry is sent, no payment is collected and no real client engagement is claimed.
