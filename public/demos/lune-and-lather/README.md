# Lune & Lather

A local natural-soap advertising concept based on the supplied alternating-layout reference. Open index.html in a browser; no build or installation is needed. Nothing has been published.

Imagined product concepts: Oat & Shea, Charcoal & Cedar, and the Botanical Trio (Oat & Shea, Charcoal & Cedar, Rose & Lavender). The supplied photos do not establish actual ingredients. Descriptions are creative proposals and must be checked against real formulations before commercial use.

Photography: Sincerely Media / Unsplash, supplied by the user. Original product photos are preserved; colour panels are applied through website CSS.
