VISUAL IDENTITY / SELF-INITIATED CONCEPT

Sift & Saffron.
A small brand, thoughtfully made.

A starter identity exploration for the fictional home bakery featured above. The need: a recognisable name across a website and Instagram. The solution: a warm wordmark, a compact profile mark and a simple palette. These are new portfolio concept assets, with no client endorsement or sales results claimed.

Colour & typography

Cocoa #432F28, cream #F7F2EA and terracotta #9E4B36 match the bakery website. The website pairs Libre Caslon Display with DM Sans; these downloadable wordmark studies use Georgia and Arial fallbacks. Use cocoa text on cream for everyday reading.

Short usage guide

Use the primary wordmark on light backgrounds and the reversed version on cocoa. Leave clear space around the logo of at least the height of its capital S. Keep the wordmark proportions intact; use the compact mark when the full name becomes too small to read. The card shows an optional design addition, quoted separately from the identity package.
