BEFORE YOU CLEAR OUT

A few good
things to know.

What do you buy?

We consider clothes, shoes and jewellery in good condition. Selection depends on condition, style and current store needs; we may not be able to buy every item.

How should I prepare my items?

Please clean your items, empty pockets and check fastenings. Keep shoe pairs together and separate delicate jewellery so it can be assessed easily.

How much will my items be worth?

We assess each item individually. Condition, quality and resale potential inform the offer. There is no obligation to accept.

Can you value everything at my house?

A home visit lets us review the collection with you. We agree the scope of the visit in advance and make an offer for eligible items after assessment.
