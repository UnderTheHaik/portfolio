INSIDE THE PROJECT

Pages & device viewsContextProblemApproachDesignDevelopmentChallengesResultWhat I learned
01

Context

A portfolio boutique concept showcasing abayas through sample products, AED prices and UAE delivery settings. It is not presented as a paying client or a live retail operation.

02

Problem

Fashion imagery needs room to breathe, while customers still need practical information about size, stock and delivery.

03

Approach

Separate everyday, occasion, signature and light-tone collections. Connect editorial imagery to the catalogue, then use product pages and a size guide to support selection.

04

Design

Burgundy, ivory and dark text create a restrained fashion identity. Large serif headings contrast with practical navigation and product labels; tall photography emphasises the garments.

05

Development

A custom WordPress/WooCommerce storefront includes variable products, catalogue filters, coupon settings and UAE shipping. The local demo supports guest checkout and simulated payment outcomes.

06

Challenges

Per-size stock, unavailable variations and shipping thresholds after discounts need clear states. Product images also need consistent proportions without cropping away important garment details.

07

Result

A responsive boutique interface and a working local commerce demo. The public site showcases the design and catalogue; account and checkout services are not available on the static preview. Products and policies are illustrative.

08

What I learned

Editorial presentation and shopping clarity have to work together. Size availability and order details deserve as much attention as the homepage.
