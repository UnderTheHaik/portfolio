دعاء الاستيقاظ

الحَمْـدُ لِلّهِ الّذي أَحْـيانا بَعْـدَ ما أَماتَـنا وَإليه النُّـشور.

All praise belongs to Allah, who restored us to life after causing us to die; to Him is the resurrection.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 6324

Source and review note

This remembrance is reported when waking.

حمد الله على العافية

الحمدُ للهِ الذي عافاني في جَسَدي وَرَدّ عَليّ روحي وَأَذِنَ لي بِذِكْرِه.

All praise belongs to Allah, who gave health to my body, returned my soul to me, and allowed me to remember Him.

Reference: Jami at-Tirmidhi 3401

Source and review note

This remembrance is reported when waking.

الذكر عند الاستيقاظ ليلاً

لا إلهَ إلاّ اللّهُ وَحْـدَهُ لا شَـريكَ له، لهُ المُلـكُ ولهُ الحَمـد، وهوَ على كلّ شيءٍ قدير، سُـبْحانَ اللهِ، والحمْـدُ لله ، ولا إلهَ إلاّ اللهُ واللهُ أكبَر، وَلا حَولَ وَلا قوّة إلاّ باللّهِ العليّ العظيم. رَبِّ اغْفرْ لي.

None is worthy of worship except Allah alone, with no partner. His is the sovereignty and His is the praise, and He has power over all things. Glory be to Allah; all praise belongs to Allah; none is worthy of worship except Allah; Allah is the Greatest; there is no power or strength except through Allah, the Most High, the Magnificent. My Lord, forgive me.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 1154

Source and review note

This remembrance is reported when waking.
