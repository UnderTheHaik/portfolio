الذكر قبل الوضوء

قبل الوضوء: بِسْمِ ٱللّٰهِ.

In the name of Allah.

Reference:

Source and review note

Mentioning Allah’s name before wudu is reported in Sunan Abi Dawud 101; grading discussions exist.

الذكر بعد الوضوء

أشْهَدُ أن لا إله إلا الله وحْدَهُ لا شريكَ لهُ ، وأشْهَدُ أنَّ محمداً عَبدُهُ ورسُولُه. اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْني مِنَ التَّوَّابينَ واجْعَلْنِي من المُتَطَهِّرِينَ. سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ وبَحَمْدكَ أشْهدُ أنْ لا إلهَ إلا أنْتَ أَسْتَغْفِرُكَ وأتُوبُ إِلَيْكَ.

I testify that none is worthy of worship except Allah alone, with no partner, and Muhammad is His servant and messenger. O Allah, make me among those who repent and those who purify themselves. Glory and praise belong to You, O Allah. I testify that none is worthy of worship except You. I ask Your forgiveness and repent to You.

Reference: Sahih Muslim 234a · Jami at-Tirmidhi 55

Source and review note

The testimony after wudu is in Sahih Muslim; the repentance/purification addition is transmitted separately. The final praise is another added formula.
