دعاء الرعـد

سبحان الله الذي يسبح الرعد بحمده والملائكة من خيفته

Glory be to Him whom the thunder glorifies with praise, and whom the angels glorify in awe.

Reference: Hisn al-Muslim 168 · Quran 13:13

Source and review note

Reported from the companion Abdullah ibn al-Zubayr on hearing thunder, not established here as a prophetic report. Hisn 168 cites al-Muwatta and al-Albani’s authentication of the companion statement. It draws on Quran 13:13 but is not the verse verbatim.

Hisn al-Muslim 168 · Quran 13:13

Personal counter default; not a claim of a prescribed repetition count.

دعاء دخول السوق

لا إله إلا الله وحده لا شريك له، له الملك وله الحمد يحيي ويميت وهو حي لا يموت بيده الخير وهو على كل شيء قدير .بسم الله، اللهم إني أسألك خير هذه السوق، وخير ما فيها، وأعوذ بك من شرها وشر ما فيها، اللهم إني أعوذ بك أن أصيب بها يميناً فاجرةً، أو صفقة خاسرة.

None is worthy of worship except Allah alone, with no partner. His is the sovereignty and His is the praise. He gives life and causes death, and He is Ever-Living and never dies. All good is in His hand, and He has power over all things. In the name of Allah. O Allah, I ask You for the good of this market and what is in it, and seek refuge in You from its evil and what is in it. I seek refuge in You from making a false oath or a losing transaction here.

Reference: Jami at-Tirmidhi 3428

Source and review note

The first formula is reported in Tirmidhi 3428, graded weak in the Darussalam edition; assessments differ. Its exceptional reward is not asserted. The second paragraph is a general request, not authenticated here as one prophetic report. No fixed count is prescribed.

Jami at-Tirmidhi 3428

Personal counter default; not a claim of a prescribed repetition count.
