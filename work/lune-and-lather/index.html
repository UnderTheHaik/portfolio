The brief and approach

Context

A self-initiated advertising concept developed from a supplied layout reference and three soap photographs by Sincerely Media. The fictional brand and product names were created for this project; ingredient blends are imaginative descriptions rather than verified formulations.

Development

A responsive static website introduces Oat & Shea, Charcoal & Cedar and the Botanical Trio. Accessible product-detail dialogs let visitors explore the imagined blends, with keyboard dismissal and focus restoration.

Result

A complete natural-soap advertising website and visual identity concept. The site does not collect orders or payments, and makes no medical or verified formulation claims.
