"ربنا ظلمنا أنفسنا وإن لم تغفر لنا وترحمنا لنكونن من الخاسرين".…

رَبَّنَا ظَلَمْنَا أَنفُسَنَا وَإِن لَّمْ تَغْفِرْ لَنَا وَتَرْحَمْنَا لَنَكُونَنَّ مِنَ الْخَاسِرِينَ.

Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves. If You do not forgive us and have mercy on us, we will surely be among the losers.

Reference: Quran 7:23

Source and review note

Prophet / teaching: Adam, with Hawwa (Eve), in Quran 7:23. Quranic words associated with the prophets. No fixed repetition count is specified by these verses.

Quran 7:23

"رب اغفر لي ولوالدي ولمن دخل بيتي مؤمنا وللمؤمنين والمؤمنات ولا تزد…

رَّبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي وَلِوَالِدَيَّ وَلِمَن دَخَلَ بَيْتِيَ مُؤْمِنًا وَلِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَالْمُؤْمِنَاتِ وَلَا تَزِدِ الظَّالِمِينَ إِلَّا تَبَارًا.رَبِّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ أَنْ أَسْأَلَكَ مَا لَيْسَ لِي بِهِ عِلْمٌ وَإِلاَّ تَغْفِرْ لِي وَتَرْحَمْنِي أَكُن مِّنَ الْخَاسِرِينَ.رَّبِّ أَنزِلْنِي مُنزَلاً مُّبَارَكاً وَأَنتَ خَيْرُ الْمُنزِلِينَ.

My Lord, forgive me, my parents, whoever enters my home as a believer, and believing men and women. Increase the wrongdoers only in ruin. My Lord, I seek refuge in You from asking You for what I do not understand. If You do not forgive me and have mercy on me, I will be among the losers. My Lord, let me arrive at a blessed place; You are the best to bring people safely to their destination.

Reference: Quran 71:28; 11:47; 23:29

Source and review note

Prophet / teaching: Nuh (Noah). Quran 71:28 and 11:47 record his prayers; 23:29 teaches him what to say when landing. Quranic words associated with the prophets. No fixed repetition count is specified by these verses.

Quran 71:28; 11:47; 23:29

"ربنا تقبل منا إنك أنت السميع العليم (127) ربنا واجعلنا مسلمين لك…

رَبَّنَا تَقَبَّلْ مِنَّا إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ رَبَّنَا وَاجْعَلْنَا مُسْلِمَيْنِ لَكَ وَمِنْ ذُرِّيَّتِنَا أُمَّةً مُسْلِمَةً لَكَ وَأَرِنَا مَنَاسِكَنَا وَتُبْ عَلَيْنَا إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ التَّوَّابُ الرَّحِيمُ .رَبِّ اجْعَلْنِي مُقِيمَ الصَّلَاةِ وَمِن ذُرِّيَّتِي ۚ رَبَّنَا وَتَقَبَّلْ دُعَاءِ رَبَّنَا اغْفِرْ لِي وَلِوَالِدَيَّ وَلِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ يَوْمَ يَقُومُ الْحِسَابُ .رَبِّ هَبْ لِي حُكْمًا وَأَلْحِقْنِي بِالصَّالِحِينَ وَاجْعَل لِّي لِسَانَ صِدْقٍ فِي الْآخِرِينَ وَاجْعَلْنِي مِن وَرَثَةِ جَنَّةِ النَّعِيمِ .رَّبَّنَا عَلَيْكَ تَوَكَّلْنَا وَإِلَيْكَ أَنَبْنَا وَإِلَيْكَ الْمَصِيرُ رَبَّنَا لَا تَجْعَلْنَا فِتْنَةً لِّلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا وَاغْفِرْ لَنَا رَبَّنَا ۖ  إِنَّكَ أَنتَ الْعَزِيزُ الْحَكِيمُ .رَبِّ هَبْ لِي مِنَ الصَّالِحِينَ.

Our Lord, accept from us; You are the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing. Make us submitted to You, and make a community submitted to You from our descendants. Show us our rites and accept our repentance; You are the Accepter of repentance, the Merciful. My Lord, make me and my descendants steadfast in prayer. Our Lord, accept my supplication. Our Lord, forgive me, my parents, and the believers on the day the reckoning is established. My Lord, grant me wisdom and join me with the righteous. Grant me honorable remembrance among later generations and make me an inheritor of the Garden of delight. Our Lord, we trust in You, return to You, and to You is our final return. Do not make us a trial for the disbelievers; forgive us. You are the Mighty, the Wise. My Lord, grant me righteous offspring.

Reference: Quran 2:127–128; 14:40–41; 26:83–85; 60:4–5; 37:100

Source and review note

Prophet / teaching: Ibrahim (Abraham). The opening prayer in 2:127–128 is shared with Ismail (Ishmael); 60:4–5 presents Ibrahim and those with him. The other cited passages are prayers of Ibrahim. Quranic words associated with the prophets. No fixed repetition count is specified by these verses.

Quran 2:127–128; 14:40–41; 26:83–85; 60:4–5; 37:100

Prophet / teaching: Hud, addressing his people in Quran 11:56. These are words expressing trust in Allah, rather than a separately prescribed ritual prayer. Quranic words associated with the prophets. No fixed repetition count is specified by these verses.

"رب إني ظلمت نفسي فاغفر لي". [القصص - 16]. "رب بما أنعمت علي فلن…

رَبِّ إِنِّي ظَلَمْتُ نَفْسِي فَاغْفِرْ لِي.رَبِّ بِمَا أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيَّ فَلَنْ أَكُونَ ظَهِيراً لِّلْمُجْرِمِينَ.رَبِّ إِنِّي لِمَا أَنزَلْتَ إِلَيَّ مِنْ خَيْرٍ فَقِيرٌ.رَبِّ اشْرَحْ لِي صَدْرِي وَيَسِّرْ لِي أَمْرِي وَاحْلُلْ عُقْدَةً مِّن لِّسَانِي يَفْقَهُوا قَوْلِي .

My Lord, I have wronged myself; forgive me. My Lord, because of Your favor upon me, I will never support the criminals. My Lord, I am in need of whatever good You send me. My Lord, open my chest, make my task easy, and loosen the knot in my tongue so they understand my speech.

Reference: Quran 28:16–17,24; 20:25–28

Source and review note

Prophet / teaching: Musa (Moses), in Quran 28:16–17, 28:24 and 20:25–28. Quranic words associated with the prophets. No fixed repetition count is specified by these verses.

Quran 28:16–17,24; 20:25–28
