أذكار الحج والعمرة

سُبْحَانَ اللهِ وَالحَمْدُ للهِ وَالله أَكْبَرُ لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْك، لَبّيْك لا شَرِيكَ لك لَبَّيْك إنَّ الحَمْدَ، والنِّعْمَةَ، لَكَ والمُلْك، لا شريك لك.   اللهُمَّ أَنْتَ السَّلَامُ وَمِنْكَ السَّلَامُ، فَحَيِّنَا رَبَّنَا بِالسَّلَامِ. بِاسْمِ اللهِ وَاللهُ أَكْبَرُ. لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَاللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، اللَّهُمَّ تَصْدِيقًا بِكِتَابِكَ، وَسُنَّةِ نَبِيِّكَ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ. رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً، وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً، وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ. اللهُمَّ رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ. لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ، بِيَدِهِ الْخَيْرُ، وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ. اللَّهُمَّ اعْصِمْنِي بِدِينِكَ، وَطَاعَةِ رَسُولِكَ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ. اللَّهُمَّ جَنِّبْنِي تَعَدِّيَ حُدُودِكَ. اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْنِي مِمَّنْ يُحِبُّكَ، وَيُحِبُّ مَلَائِكَتَكَ، وَرُسُلَكَ، وَعِبَادَكَ الصَّالِحِينَ اللَّهُمَّ حَبِّبْنِي إِلَيْكَ، وَإِلَى مَلَائِكَتِكَ، وَرُسُلِكَ، وَعِبَادَكَ الصَّالِحِينَ. اللَّهُمَّ آتِنِي مِنْ خَيْرِ مَا تُؤْتِي عِبَادَكَ الصَّالِحِينَ فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةِ. اللَّهُمَّ يَسِّرْنِي لِلْيُسْرَى، وَجَنِّبْنِي الْعُسْرَى، وَاغْفِرْ. نَبْدَأُ بِمَا بَدَأَ اللَّهُ بِهِ. إِنَّ الصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةَ مِنْ شَعَائِرِ اللَّهِ. لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ، لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللهُ، أَنْجَزَ وَعْدَهُ، وَنَصَرَ عَبْدَهُ، وَهَزَمَ الْأَحْزَابَ وَحْدَهُ. اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ قُلْتَ: {اِدْعُونِي أَسْتَجِبْ لَكُمْ} وَإِنَّكَ لاَ تُخْلِفُ الْمِيعَادَ، وَإِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ كَمَا هَدَيْتَنِي إِلَى الإِسْلاَمِ أَلاَ تَنْزِعَهُ مِنِّي حَتَّى تَتَوَفَّانِي، وَأَنَا مُسْلِمٌ. اللَّهُمَّ اعْصِمْنَا بِدِينِكَ، وَطَوَاعِيَتِكَ، وَطَوَاعِيَةِ رَسُولِكَ، وَجَنِّبْنَا تَعَدِّيَ حُدُودِكَ. اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْنَا نُحِبُّكَ، وَنُحِبُّ مَلَائِكَتَكَ، وَأَنْبِيَاءَكَ، وَرُسَلَكَ، وَنُحِبُّ عِبَادَكَ الصَّالِحِينَ. اللَّهُمَّ حَبِّبْنَا إِلَيْكَ وَإِلَى مَلَائِكَتِكَ، وَإِلَى أَنْبِيَائِكَ، وَرُسُلِكَ، وَإِلَى عِبَادِكَ الصَّالِحِينَ. اللَّهُمَّ يَسِّرْنَا لِلْيُسْرَى، وَجَنِّبْنَا الْعُسْرَى، وَاغْفِرْ لَنَا فِي الْآخِرَةِ. رَبِّ اغْفِرْ وَارْحَمْ إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ الْأَعَزُّ الْأَكْرَمُ. لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ، وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ. لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ، وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ. اللَّهُمَّ اهْدِنَا بِالْهُدَى، وَزَيِّنَّا بِالتَّقْوَى، وَاغْفِرْ لَنَا فِي الْآخِرَةِ وَالْأُولَى. اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ مِنْ فَضْلِكَ وَعَطَائِكَ رِزْقًا طَيِّبًا مُبَارَكًا. اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ أَمَرْتَ بِالدُّعَاءِ، وَقَضَيْتَ عَلَى نَفْسِكَ بِالِاسْتِجَابَةِ، وَأَنْتَ لَا تُخْلِفُ وَعْدَكَ، وَلَا تَكْذِبُ عَهْدَكَ، اللَّهُمَّ مَا أَحْبَبْتَ مِنْ خَيْرٍ فَحَبِّبْهُ إِلَيْنَا، وَيَسِّرْهُ لَنَا، وَمَا كَرِهْتَ مِنْ شَيْءٍ فَكَرِّهْهُ إِلَيْنَا وَجَنِّبْنَاهُ، وَلَا تَنْزِعْ عَنَّا الْإِسْلَامَ بَعْدَ إِذْ أَعْطَيْتَنَا. الله أَكْبَر، الله أَكْبَر، الله أَكْبَر. اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْهُ حَجًّا مَبْرُورًا وَذَنْبًا مَغْفُورًا. بسم الله , اللهم تقبل من محمد وآل محمد ومن أمة محمد الله أَكْبَر، الله أَكْبَر، الله أَكْبَر. لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ، وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ، وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ، آيِبُونَ، تَائِبُونَ، عَابِدُونَ، سَاجِدُونَ. اَللّٰهُمَّ اجْعَلْهُ حَجًّا مَبْرُوْرًا وَسَعْيًا مَشْكُوْرًا وَذَنْبًا مَغْفُوْرًا.

Glory be to Allah; all praise belongs to Allah; Allah is the Greatest. Here I am, O Allah, here I am. Here I am, with no partner to You. Praise, favor, and sovereignty belong to You, with no partner. O Allah, You are Peace and peace comes from You; greet us with peace. In the name of Allah; Allah is the Greatest. None is worthy of worship except Allah; Allah is the Greatest. O Allah, I affirm Your Book and the way of Your Prophet. Our Lord, grant good in this world and the next and protect us from the Fire. None is worthy of worship except Allah alone, with no partner. His is the sovereignty and His is the praise, All good is in His hand, and He has power over all things. O Allah, protect me through Your religion and obedience to Your Messenger. Keep me from transgressing Your limits. Make me love You, Your angels, messengers, and righteous servants, and make me beloved to You and to them. Give me the good You give Your righteous servants in this world and the next. Make ease accessible to me, keep hardship away, and forgive me. We begin with what Allah began with. Safa and Marwah are among Allah’s sacred symbols. Allah is the Greatest. None is worthy of worship except Allah alone, with no partner. His is the sovereignty and His is the praise, and He has power over all things. None is worthy of worship except Allah, who fulfilled His promise, supported His servant, and defeated the confederates alone. O Allah, You said, “Call upon Me; I will answer you,” and You do not break promises. As You guided me to Islam, do not remove it from me until You take me as a Muslim. Protect us through Your religion, obedience to You and Your Messenger, and keep us from transgressing Your limits. Make us love You, Your angels, prophets, messengers, and righteous servants, and make us beloved to You and them. Make ease accessible, keep hardship away, and forgive us in the hereafter. My Lord, forgive and have mercy; You are the Mightiest, the Most Generous. None is worthy of worship except Allah alone, with no partner. His is the sovereignty and His is the praise, and He has power over all things. None is worthy of worship except Allah alone, with no partner. His is the sovereignty and His is the praise, and He has power over all things. O Allah, guide us with guidance, adorn us with piety, and forgive us in this world and the next. Grant wholesome, blessed provision from Your favor and gifts. You commanded prayer and promised to answer; You do not break promises or covenants. Make good beloved and easy for us; make what You dislike hateful to us and keep it away. Do not remove Islam from us after giving it. Allah is the Greatest, three times. O Allah, make it an accepted pilgrimage and forgive sins. In the name of Allah. O Allah, accept from Muhammad, his family, and his community. Allah is the Greatest, three times. None is worthy of worship except Allah alone, with no partner. His is the sovereignty and His is the praise, and He has power over all things. We return, repenting, worshipping, and prostrating. O Allah, make it an accepted pilgrimage, appreciated striving, and forgiven sin.

Reference: Sahih Muslim 1184b · Sahih Muslim 1218a · Quran 2:201

Source and review note

This is a compilation. The talbiyah and Safa/Marwah remembrance are narrated; the additional prayers are not one fixed prophetic formula.
