Domain & hosting

We choose the standard domain together, with your approval, and register it in your name. A specific name has no extra fee unless its domain cost exceeds the included 75 AED allowance. A suitable free static hosting tier may be used; any paid hosting is quoted separately. Hosting setup does not include maintenance.

Included with every build

Website prices use text and photos you provide. Every build includes 14 days of fixes for faults in my delivered work. Social setup uses your existing branding; ongoing posts, ads and account management are separate.

Website care terms

Care is optional and can be cancelled before the next billing month. Update time includes reviewing, editing, checking and publishing. Requests are grouped into one monthly batch for Care or two for Care Plus; unused time does not roll over. Routine updates are handled within three working days.

Requests beyond a care package

New pages, redesigns, features and third-party charges are quoted separately. There is no emergency or 24/7 support promise. Any new work is agreed before it begins.

BEFORE WE BEGIN

Your website questions, answered.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every website includes a responsive layout, with the main pages and visitor journeys checked on desktop and phone screens.

How much does a website cost?

Introductory prices start at 350 AED for a one-page website and 500 AED for a simple business website. The 1,000 AED launch bundle combines a website and starter visual identity. Domain and provider costs are quoted separately.

How do we get started?

Ready to begin? Send me your business details, the pages you need, your budget and your preferred launch date. We agree on a written scope and quote before work starts.

Can you create my logo and colours too?

Yes. Logo design on its own is 300 AED and includes two design directions, two revision rounds and final files. The expanded 600 AED starter identity adds a colour palette, font pairing, short guidelines and a profile image.

What happens after launch?

You receive the agreed files and account access. You have 14 days to report faults in the agreed work. Content updates are quoted before work begins.
