INSIDE THE PROJECT

Pages & device viewsContextProblemApproachDesignDevelopmentChallengesResultWhat I learned
01

Context

A self-initiated concept for a fictional UAE lifestyle store, bringing scarves, stationery, prayer essentials and gifts into one catalogue. This is a portfolio project, not a client business.

02

Problem

A varied catalogue needs a clear route from discovering a collection to choosing a product, without losing the quiet character of the brand.

03

Approach

Organise products into four collections, use consistent product cards and place prices, variations and delivery information close to the purchase decision.

04

Design

Soft olive, warm neutral backgrounds and generous spacing support an unhurried browsing experience. System sans-serif and Georgia keep typography readable without external font requests. Product photography provides the visual focus.

05

Development

A custom WordPress theme wraps WooCommerce catalogue, cart and account templates. PHP handles catalogue filters and product information; JavaScript adds saved-product controls. Optimised WebP photographs are served locally.

06

Challenges

Product variations, stock, shipping and discounts need consistent behaviour across the shopping journey. The local demo uses WooCommerce calculations rather than recreating commerce logic in the browser.

07

Result

A responsive storefront with collections, product details and a local test checkout. The public GitHub Pages site is a static design preview: checkout, accounts and server filtering require the local runtime. No real payments or sales are claimed.

08

What I learned

A polished shop also depends on the less visible details: variation states, delivery thresholds and clear explanations of what a demo can actually do.
