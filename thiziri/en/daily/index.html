سبحان الله

سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ

Glory be to Allah.

Reference: Sahih Muslim 2698

Source and review note

The Prophet ﷺ described glorifying Allah one hundred times in a day.

Sahih Muslim 2698

يُسَبِّحُ مِائَةَ تَسْبِيحَةٍ، فَيُكْتَبُ لَهُ أَلْفُ حَسَنَةٍ أَوْ يُحَطُّ عَنْهُ أَلْفُ خَطِيئَةٍ

Counter target: 100

سبحان الله والحمد لله

سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ

Glory be to Allah, and all praise belongs to Allah.

Reference: Sahih Muslim 223

Source and review note

The narration praises this remembrance; it does not prescribe one hundred repetitions.

Sahih Muslim 223

سبحان الله العظيم وبحمده

سُبْحَانَ اللهِ العَظِيمِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ

Glory and praise belong to Allah, the Magnificent.

Reference: Jami at-Tirmidhi 3464

Source and review note

Tirmidhi calls the palm-tree report hasan sahih gharib; the Darussalam edition grades its chain weak. Grading differs. The benefit is not presented as undisputed, and no fixed count is specified.

Jami at-Tirmidhi 3464

Personal counter default; not a claim of a prescribed repetition count.

سبحان الله وبحمده ، سبحان الله العظيم

سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ ، سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ الْعَظِيمِ

Glory and praise belong to Allah. Glory be to Allah, the Magnificent.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 6406

Source and review note

These two phrases are described as light on the tongue and weighty in the balance. No fixed count is specified.

اللهم صل وسلم وبارك على سيدنا محمد

الْلَّهُم صَلِّ وَسَلِم وَبَارِك عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّد

O Allah, send blessings, peace, and blessing upon our master Muhammad.

Reference: Sahih Muslim 408

Source and review note

One blessing upon the Prophet ﷺ brings ten blessings from Allah. This general salawat wording is not a verbatim quotation of that narration, and it specifies no count of one hundred.

Sahih Muslim 408

مَنْ صَلَّى عَلَيَّ وَاحِدَةً صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ عَشْرًا

Glory be to Allah. All praise belongs to Allah. None is worthy of worship except Allah. Allah is the Greatest. O Allah, forgive me, have mercy on me, and provide for me.

Source and review note

The remembrance and requests for forgiveness, mercy, and provision are supported separately; this is a combined reading.

Sahih Muslim 2695 · Sahih Muslim 2696
