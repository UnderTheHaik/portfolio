# Fern & Flour

A separate café and bakery website concept based on the supplied sage-green reference and sixteen supplied Unsplash photographs. Open index.html to browse locally. No installation or build is required.

Features: searchable menu, five category filters, product-detail dialogs, allergen notes, browser-saved favourites and responsive navigation. No orders or payments are sent. Brand, menu recipes, prices and Dubai setting are illustrative.

The hero composition uses the supplied coffee and croissant photographs edited with AI to remove their backgrounds and combine them on a plate. Original files remain untouched. The other photographs retain their original backgrounds and are resized for the site.

Photographers: shche_ team, Fanny Gustafsson, Paulina Chmolowska, Johanna, Jasmin Egger, Jay, Brett Wharton, Precious Madubuike, Rumman Amin, Samedy Nguon, Tabitha Turner, Caleb, Hrushi Chavhan and Jarek Ceborski, via Unsplash. Files supplied by the user.
