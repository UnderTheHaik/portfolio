Café homepage

The brief and approach

Context

A self-initiated café concept adapted from a supplied sage-green layout reference and sixteen supplied Unsplash photographs. Branding, Dubai setting, sample recipes and AED prices are illustrative.

Development

A responsive sixteen-item menu supports category filters, text search, product-detail dialogs, illustrative allergen notes and favourites saved in the visitor’s browser. Mobile navigation adapts the layout for phone screens.

Result

A complete café and bakery portfolio demo with working browser interactions. No orders, payments or bookings are sent.
