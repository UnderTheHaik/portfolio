ما يقال عند زيارة المريض

لَا بَأْسَ، طَهُورٌ إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ.

No harm; may it be a purification, if Allah wills.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 3616

Source and review note

No fixed repetition count is specified in this report. The displayed target is a personal counter.

Sahih al-Bukhari 3616

الدعاء للمريض سبع مرات

أَسْأَلُ اللَّهَ الْعَظِيمَ رَبَّ الْعَرْشِ الْعَظِيمِ أَنْ يَشْفِيَكَ.

I ask Allah, the Magnificent, Lord of the magnificent Throne, to heal you.

Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud 3106

Source and review note

Reported when visiting a sick person whose appointed time has not come, seven times. Graded sahih by al-Albani in the cited edition; the app does not promise a particular medical outcome.

Sunan Abi Dawud 3106

Counter target: 7

دعاء الشفاء

اللَّهُمَّ رَبَّ النَّاسِ أَذْهِبِ الْبَأْسَ، اشْفِهِ وَأَنْتَ الشَّافِي، لَا شِفَاءَ إِلَّا شِفَاؤُكَ، شِفَاءً لَا يُغَادِرُ سَقَمًا.

O Allah, Lord of people, remove the suffering and heal him. You are the Healer. There is no healing except Your healing, a healing that leaves no illness.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 5743

Source and review note

No fixed repetition count is specified in this report. The displayed target is a personal counter.

Sahih al-Bukhari 5743

الدعاء إذا أحسست بوجع في جسدك ضع يدك على الذي تألم من جسدك وقل: بسم…

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ. أَعُوذُ بِاللَّهِ وَقُدْرَتِهِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا أَجِدُ وَأُحَاذِرُ.

In the name of Allah. I seek refuge in Allah and His power from the evil of what I feel and fear.

Reference: Sahih Muslim 2202

Source and review note

Place a hand on the painful area; say Bismillah three times, then the refuge formula seven times. Mark the whole sequence read when finished. Sahih Muslim 2202.

Sahih Muslim 2202
