آية الكرسي

أَعُوذُ بِاللهِ مِنْ الشَّيْطَانِ الرَّجِيمِ اللّهُ لاَ إِلَـهَ إِلاَّ هُوَ الْحَيُّ الْقَيُّومُ لاَ تَأْخُذُهُ سِنَةٌ وَلاَ نَوْمٌ لَّهُ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَمَا فِي الأَرْضِ مَن ذَا الَّذِي يَشْفَعُ عِنْدَهُ إِلاَّ بِإِذْنِهِ يَعْلَمُ مَا بَيْنَ أَيْدِيهِمْ وَمَا خَلْفَهُمْ وَلاَ يُحِيطُونَ بِشَيْءٍ مِّنْ عِلْمِهِ إِلاَّ بِمَا شَاء وَسِعَ كُرْسِيُّهُ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالأَرْضَ وَلاَ يَؤُودُهُ حِفْظُهُمَا وَهُوَ الْعَلِيُّ الْعَظِيمُ. .

I seek refuge in Allah from Satan, the accursed. Allah: none is worthy of worship except Him, the Ever-Living, the Sustainer. Neither drowsiness nor sleep overtakes Him. Everything in the heavens and on earth belongs to Him. Who can intercede with Him without His permission? He knows what lies before them and behind them. They grasp nothing of His knowledge except what He wills. His Kursi encompasses the heavens and earth, and preserving them does not tire Him. He is the Most High, the Magnificent.

Reference: Quran 2:255

Source and review note

Ayat al-Kursi is Quranic text. The verified bedtime protection report is in Sahih al-Bukhari 2311.

Quran 2:255

Personal counter default; not a claim of a prescribed repetition count.

سورة الإخلاص

بِسْمِ اللهِ الرَّحْمنِ الرَّحِيم قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ، ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ، لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ، وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۢ.

In the name of Allah, the Most Merciful, the Especially Merciful. Say: He is Allah, the One. Allah, the One upon whom all depend. He neither begets nor was born, and none is comparable to Him.

Reference: Quran 112:1–4 · Sunan Abi Dawud 5082

سورة الفلق

Reference: Quran 113:1–5 · Sunan Abi Dawud 5082

سورة الناس

بِسْمِ اللهِ الرَّحْمنِ الرَّحِيم قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلنَّاسِ، مَلِكِ ٱلنَّاسِ، إِلَٰهِ ٱلنَّاسِ، مِن شَرِّ ٱلْوَسْوَاسِ ٱلْخَنَّاسِ، ٱلَّذِى يُوَسْوِسُ فِى صُدُورِ ٱلنَّاسِ، مِنَ ٱلْجِنَّةِ وَٱلنَّاسِ.

أصبحنا وأصبح الملك لله والحمد لله ، لا إله إلا الله وحده لا شريك…

أَصْـبَحْنا وَأَصْـبَحَ المُـلْكُ لله وَالحَمدُ لله ، لا إلهَ إلاّ اللّهُ وَحدَهُ لا شَريكَ لهُ، لهُ المُـلكُ ولهُ الحَمْـد، وهُوَ على كلّ شَيءٍ قدير ، رَبِّ أسْـأَلُـكَ خَـيرَ ما في هـذا اليوم وَخَـيرَ ما بَعْـدَه ، وَأَعـوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَـرِّ ما في هـذا اليوم وَشَرِّ ما بَعْـدَه، رَبِّ أَعـوذُبِكَ مِنَ الْكَسَـلِ وَسـوءِ الْكِـبَر ، رَبِّ أَعـوذُ بِكَ مِنْ عَـذابٍ في النّـارِ وَعَـذابٍ في القَـبْر.

We have entered the morning, and all sovereignty belongs to Allah. All praise belongs to Allah. None is worthy of worship except Allah alone, with no partner. His is the sovereignty and His is the praise, and He has power over all things. My Lord, I ask You for the good of this day and what follows it, and seek refuge in You from its evil and what follows it. My Lord, I seek refuge in You from laziness and the hardships of old age, from punishment in the Fire and punishment in the grave.

Reference: Sahih Muslim 2723a

Source and review note

This supplication is reported for evening, with the corresponding wording used in the morning.

سيد الاستغفار

اللّهـمَّ أَنْتَ رَبِّـي لا إلهَ إلاّ أَنْتَ ، خَلَقْتَنـي وَأَنا عَبْـدُك ، وَأَنا عَلـى عَهْـدِكَ وَوَعْـدِكَ ما اسْتَـطَعْـت ، أَعـوذُبِكَ مِنْ شَـرِّ ما صَنَـعْت ، أَبـوءُ لَـكَ بِنِعْـمَتِـكَ عَلَـيَّ وَأَبـوءُ بِذَنْـبي فَاغْفـِرْ لي فَإِنَّـهُ لا يَغْـفِرُ الذُّنـوبَ إِلاّ أَنْتَ .

O Allah, You are my Lord; none is worthy of worship except You. You created me and I am Your servant. I keep Your covenant and promise as best I can. I seek refuge in You from the evil I have done. I acknowledge Your favor upon me and confess my sin. Forgive me, for none forgives sins except You.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 6306

Source and review note

Sayyid al-Istighfar: a supplication the Prophet ﷺ taught for sincere seeking of forgiveness.

Sahih al-Bukhari 6306

I seek refuge in You from the evil of my own soul, from the evil of Satan and his association of partners with You, and from committing wrong against myself or bringing it upon another Muslim.

Reference: Jami at-Tirmidhi 3392

Source and review note

The main supplication is narrated for morning, evening, and bedtime. The final request is found in another transmitted version; this combined version has not been checked against a single report.

Jami at-Tirmidhi 3392
