02 / A SIMPLE PRACTICE

One remembrance.
One moment at a time.

Thiziri keeps the reading itself at the centre. Open a collection, move through it one dhikr at a time, and save the words you want to return to.

01

Words and their meaning

Arabic is always present. English and French meanings follow your chosen language. Source and review notes open when you need them.

02

Progress at your pace

Tap once per recitation, move with Previous and Next, and keep favourites in this browser. Daily counters start fresh on the next local date.

03

Room to come back

After the site has finished saving its reading files, you can return offline in the same browser. No sign-in or subscription is required.
