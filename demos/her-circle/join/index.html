A FEW THINGS WE SHARE

Our community
guidelines

  • Women-only space. Please respect the purpose of the circle.
  • Be kind and inclusive. Welcome new faces and respect different backgrounds.
  • Ask before sharing. Get permission before posting photos of others or passing on contact details.
  • Keep conversations relevant. No unsolicited sales messages or promotions.
  • RSVP thoughtfully. If your plans change, tell the host as early as you can.
  • Respect personal boundaries. Nobody needs to explain a preference or join an activity they’re uncomfortable with.

A LITTLE REASSURANCE

Before your first hello

Can I come on my own?

Absolutely. Many people join communities to meet someone new. A host can help with introductions so you don’t have to figure it all out alone.

Do I have to attend everything?

No. Choose the gatherings you enjoy and join when it suits you.

Are the gatherings free?

It depends on the activity. Walks might have no activity fee, while workshops, brunches, galas and spa treatments can have costs. The host should share the price and cancellation terms before any booking.

What if I have accessibility needs?

Ask the host about the venue, facilities and activity before confirming. These details should be provided for each gathering.

Are there real events to book here?

This website is a demo concept. Its programme is illustrative, and there are no live bookings or payments.
